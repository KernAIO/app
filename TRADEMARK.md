# Trademark policy

The Kern **code** is open source — see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). The Kern **name and marks** are
not. This file says what you may do with them.

The marks are the word **Kern**, the word **KernAIO**, the Kern mark (`assets/kern-mark.svg`), and the
domain **kernaio.com**. They belong to KernAIO.

Trademark and copyright are separate. Your right to run, modify and redistribute the code does not
come with a right to use the name — and nothing here takes away any right the licence gives you.

## You do not need our permission to

- Run Kern, modified or not, and say that you run Kern.
- Say that your product **works with**, **integrates with**, or is **built on** Kern.
- Name a module `kern-something` or `something-for-kern`, and say it is a module for Kern.
- Write about Kern, review it, teach it, and use the name in articles, talks and comparisons.
- Use the mark to link to us, unmodified and at a sensible size.
- Fork the code and keep the notices intact in the source. You must rename the product itself — see
  below.

## You do need our permission to

- Call your hosted service **Kern**, **Kern Cloud**, or anything a reasonable person would read as us
  — `kern.example.com`, "Kern Hosting", "Managed Kern".
- Register a domain, org, app-store listing or company name containing **Kern** or **KernAIO**.
- Use the mark as your own product's logo, or a modified version of it as any logo.
- Suggest that KernAIO endorses, supports or certifies you when we have not.

## Forks

Fork freely. Change the product name, the visible branding and the domain. Keep the copyright and
licence notices in the source — that is a licence obligation, not a trademark one, and removing them
is not allowed.

"A fork of Kern" is an accurate description and you may use it. "Kern" as the name of your fork is
not.

## Enforcement

If a use confuses people about who made or runs the software, we will ask you to change it, and we
will ask before doing anything else. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask first —
that is cheaper for both of us than finding out later.
